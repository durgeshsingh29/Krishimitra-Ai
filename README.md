# 🌾 KrishiMitra AI

## 🚜 Problem Statement

Small and marginal farmers often face challenges such as:

- Lack of timely crop disease detection
- Uncertain irrigation decisions
- Fluctuating market prices
- Limited access to expert agricultural guidance
- Difficulty understanding satellite and weather data

These issues lead to reduced productivity, higher costs, and lower profits.

---

## 💡 Solution

KrishiMitra AI is an AI-powered smart farming assistant that helps farmers make better decisions using:

- Voice-based AI assistance in multiple languages
- Crop disease detection
- Satellite-based crop monitoring
- Smart irrigation recommendations
- Real-time weather insights
- Market price predictions and selling suggestions

The platform combines multiple AI agents to provide actionable recommendations for farmers.
